# Hide API

> Hide (Heaps IDE) is the editor of the Heaps engine. `hrt` is its runtime, used by the games: prefabs (3D levels, FX, materials, render props...), shader graphs, animation graphs and texture graphs. `hide` is the editor itself (JS/nw.js and HashLink versions): views, property editors, CastleDB editor and editing tools of the prefabs.

Each link points to the Markdown page of a type: signatures, documentation, inheritance and platform availability.
Full text of all the pages: https://jonathan-heteau.github.io/heaps-docs/llms/hide-full.txt
